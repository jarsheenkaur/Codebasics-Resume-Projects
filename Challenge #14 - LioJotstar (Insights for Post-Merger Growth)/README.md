# LioJotstar: Data-Backed Strategy for Leading Digital Streaming Industry in India   

- Analyzed LioCinema and Jotstar data to **support a strategic merger** by providing insights into **content libraries**, **subscriber trends**, **user inactivity**, **subscription changes**, and **content consumption**, with a **strong emphasis on data visualization**. 
- Utilized **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)** for data cleaning, transformation, and visualization within **Jupyter Notebooks** to **identify key patterns and correlations related to user behavior and platform performance**. 
- Generated actionable recommendations to **optimize content strategies and user engagement**, contributing to the goal of **establishing the merged platform as the leading OTT service**.

## Project Features & Highlights

- Analyzed structured data from two OTT platforms, **LioCinema and Jotstar**, both using **identical schema, table names, and column formats**.
- Built a **modular Jupyter notebook workflow** to handle the entire pipeline from **data loading, cleaning, and transformation to insight generation**.
- Stored **intermediate data in Parquet format** to allow **seamless reuse and consistency** across multiple notebooks and stages.
- Performed **feature engineering** by creating derived fields like **Subscription Month, Plan Change Type, and Plan Transition** to extract behavioral patterns.
- Conducted **multi-dimensional analysis** aligned to **seven key business objectives** including **content diversity**, **subscription behavior**, **engagement trends**, **upgrade/downgrade movement**, **watch patterns**, and **monetization**.
- Formulated key **platform-level KPIs and metrics** to compare **user scale, plan conversions, activity rates, average watch time**, and **ARPU** between both platforms.
- Extracted **data-driven insights** that directly informed strategic focus areas including **user segmentation**, **city-tier expansion**, **regional content scaling**, **long-form and daily engagement formats**, **upgrade optimization**, and **differentiated pricing**.
- Translated insights into **executable strategies** across **platform unification**, **telecom bundling**, **dynamic plan tiers**, **ambassador selection**, **AI-driven personalization**, **live sports monetization**, and **digital event innovation**.
- Ensured every strategy proposed is **implementation-ready**, **scalable**, and tailored to position **LioJotstar as India’s most dominant OTT platform post-merger**.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

1. **Total Content Items** – The total number of unique content titles available on the platform, including movies, series, and specials.
2. **Total Users** – The cumulative number of users registered or subscribed to the platform.
3. **Paid Users** – The number of users currently on any paid subscription plan.
4. **Paid Users %** – The percentage of total users who are paid subscribers.
5. **Active Users** – Users who have shown recent activity, indicated by a valid Last Active Date.
6. **Inactive Users** – Users with no recorded activity within the defined period.
7. **Active Rate (%)** – The percentage of users who are currently active on the platform.
8. **Inactive Rate (%)** – The percentage of users who are currently inactive on the platform.
9. **Upgraded Users** – Users who have moved from a lower-value plan to a higher-value plan.
10. **Upgrade Rate (%)** – The percentage of users who upgraded their subscription plan.
11. **Downgraded Users** – Users who have moved from a higher-value plan to a lower-value plan.
12. **Downgrade Rate (%)** – The percentage of users who downgraded their subscription plan.
13. **Total Watch Time (hrs)** – The total number of hours streamed by users on the platform.
14. **Average Watch Time (hrs)** – The average number of hours watched per user.
15. **Upgrade vs. Downgrade Rate (%)** – The ratio of upgrade rate to downgrade rate, expressed as a percentage.
16. **Total Revenue** – The total amount of revenue generated from all paid users.
17. **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)** – The average revenue earned per user, calculated across the entire user base.
18. **Monthly Users Growth Rate (%)** – The percentage change in user base compared to the previous month.
19. **Monthly Upgrade Volume Growth Rate (%)** – The month-over-month percentage change in the number of users who upgraded their plan.
20. **Monthly Downgrade Volume Growth Rate (%)** – The month-over-month percentage change in the number of users who downgraded their plan.
21. **Monthly Upgrade vs. Downgrade Rate (%)** – The ratio of monthly upgrade rate to downgrade rate, expressed as a percentage.
22. **Monthly Revenue Growth Rate (%)** – The percentage increase or decrease in revenue compared to the previous month.
